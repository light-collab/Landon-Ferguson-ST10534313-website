About Nexus Pro

How we work.

Nexus Pro started with one idea: a good business should never lose customers just because it is hard to find online. I am Landon Ferguson, and I build the websites and systems that put local businesses in front of the people already looking for them, from Strand across the Western Cape.

  1. Listen. We agree on your goals and what success looks like.
  2. Design. You see the layout before anything is built.
  3. Build. Fast, mobile-friendly pages with clean code.
  4. Launch and support. We publish, test everything and stay available.
  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the foundations.
  • Leaflet and OpenStreetMap for interactive maps.
  • GitHub to keep every change on record, and Netlify to publish.

Nothing exotic: tools that are fast, secure and easy to maintain. See the full list of services.

Why it matters

Without a website, you are not in the room.

Customers look you up on their phone before they call, visit or buy. If they cannot find you, or what they find looks neglected, they move on to someone they can.

That is why I built Nexus Pro. You do not need to suffer in silence, wondering what you can do about this. Take my hand and let us tackle it together.
